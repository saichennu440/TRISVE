Decades of Expertise

With over 15 years of experience in the education sector, Trisve works closely with students, providing personalized one-on-one counseling and training. We offer clear, step-by-step roadmaps to help students navigate the complexities of global education.

The Visionary

Veena Kulur

"Studying abroad is not just an academic decision — it is an emotional one."

Veena Kulur, Founder of Trisve, has spent over 15 years helping students unlock international opportunities. Driven by her own journey and an unwavering commitment to excellence, Veena established Trisve as a sanctuary for student growth.

Her philosophy is simple: Global education should be accessible, transparent, and transformative. Under her leadership, Trisve has evolved from a boutique consultancy into a trusted name for elite international placements, known for its personalized touch and high success rates.

Beyond the paperwork and applications, Veena's vision is to build a bridge for students to discover their best selves in a global context. She personally oversees the training programs, ensuring that every Trisve student carries the values of integrity and ambition wherever they go.
